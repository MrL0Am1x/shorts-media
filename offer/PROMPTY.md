# Видео «Предложение»: картинки для генерации

Где делать: ChatGPT (бесплатно) или Gemini. Всё в ОДНОМ чате, по порядку — тогда персонаж будет одинаковым.

Сначала отправь это сообщение (один раз):

```
I will ask you for 7 vertical images (9:16) for one animated story. Keep the SAME main character in every image:
a 20-year-old guy with messy dark hair, light-brown skin, round thin glasses, oversized mustard-yellow hoodie.
Style for all images: cinematic digital illustration, semi-realistic cartoon, soft painterly shading,
warm moody lighting, detailed background, like a frame from a modern animated film. No text, no letters, no watermarks.
Leave the top third of the image calm (dark sky, wall or ceiling) — I will put captions there.
```

Потом по одной картинке (после каждой жди результат):

1. `Image 1: night, 3 AM, dark bedroom, the guy lies in bed under a blanket, his face lit by a smartphone screen, blue moonlight from the window.`

2. `Image 2: close-up of the guy's hands holding the smartphone in the dark, the screen glows bright, his surprised face is blurred in the background.`

3. `Image 3: the guy sits on the bed, laughing in disbelief, staring at the phone, the screen shows a green banking notification glow, warm lamp light.`

4. `Image 4: the guy puts the turned-off phone into a wooden desk drawer and locks it with a small key, calm morning sunlight.`

5. `Image 5: day 12, rainy grey afternoon, the guy sits on the floor by the window looking nervously at the locked drawer, the room is messy, lonely atmosphere.`

6. `Image 6: night, the locked desk drawer glows from inside with blue light as if a phone is ringing inside, the guy stands frozen in the dark doorway, tense dramatic lighting.`

7. `Image 7: extreme close-up, the guy's trembling hand hovers over the key in the drawer lock, his worried face in soft focus, dramatic cinematic light.`

Сохрани все 7 картинок и пришли мне по порядку (1–7). Остальное сделаю я: анимацию, денежный счётчик, субтитры, звук и музыку.
